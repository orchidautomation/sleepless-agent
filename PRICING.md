- Their CRM, their calendar, their email

- Simplest to deliver

*Marketing team, Sales ops, etc.*

- Team-specific prompt
- Anyone on team can use it

**Price:** Base + 20%

---

### Team (Per-User Connections)
*Each person connects their own apps*

- User onboarding flow required
- Composio multi-tenant (userId per person)
- Personal email/calendar + shared CRM

**Price:** Base + 40-50%

**Ideal:**
- Series A/B startup, 20-100 employees

- Has budget ($15-50k is not a big decision)

## Objection Handling

**"Why not just use Lindy/Gumloop?"**
> "Those are credit-based and limited. You hit limits, can't customize, no code access. This is yours — runs on your Vercel, your Composio account, your Slack. No vendor lock-in. And I tune it to your specific workflows."

**"Can we do a pilot first?"**
> "Yes — let's start with just you and one other person. 3-month minimum. If it works, we expand to the team."

**"That's expensive"**
> "It's 3-4 weeks of my time, custom to you, and I maintain it monthly. Hiring someone to build this in-house would take 3-6 months and cost $150k+/year. Or you can use Lindy at $200/mo and hit walls within a month."

**"What if it doesn't work?"**
> "I've built this for myself — it runs 24/7. The risk is low. But if after 30 days you're not seeing value, we'll figure it out together."

## Sales Process

1. **Intro call** (30 min)
   - Understand their pain
   - Show your own OS briefly
   - Qualify: budget, timeline, decision maker

2. **Scoping call** (45 min)
   - Map their workflows
   - Identify 3-5 key use cases
   - Confirm integrations needed

3. **Proposal** (async)
   - Scope summary
   - Price + timeline
   - What's included

4. **Close**
   - 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
   - 3-month retainer minimum
   - Sign and kick off
